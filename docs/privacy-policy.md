# Chess Review — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2 October 2026 · Publisher: Sangeeta Studios_

Chess Review analyses chess games entirely on your device.

- **No data collection.** The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or usage data. It does not request the Internet permission.
- **Games you import** (pasted, shared from another app, or opened from a file) and their analysis are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Deleting a game in the app, clearing app data, or uninstalling removes them.
- **Settings** (your username for board orientation and the analysis strength) are stored only on your device.
- **Backups.** If you have Android backup enabled, your imported games and settings may be included in your device backup, which is handled by Google under your Google account settings.
- **No third-party SDKs, ads, analytics or tracking.**
- **Children.** The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone.

Questions: contact the developer through the Google Play listing.
